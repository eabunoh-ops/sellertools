How to reduce image size without losing quality

Large photos slow down your store and hurt sales. Resizing to a sensible width (1200 to 1600 px is plenty for most product pages) and using a quality of 70 to 80 percent usually cuts file size by more than half, with no visible difference.

Frequently asked questions

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything happens inside your browser, so your images stay on your device.

Which format is best for a store?

WebP gives smaller files at the same quality and works in all modern browsers. JPEG is the safest choice if you need wide compatibility.

What quality setting should I use?

Start at 75 percent. Lower it for smaller files, raise it if you notice blurriness.
