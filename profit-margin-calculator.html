How profit margin is calculated

Profit = selling price - (product cost + shipping and other costs + fees).

Margin = profit / selling price x 100. Markup = profit / total cost x 100. Margin tells you how much of each sale you keep. Markup tells you how much you add on top of your cost.

Frequently asked questions

What is a good profit margin for online sellers?

It depends on the product. Many online stores aim for a net margin of 15 to 30 percent after ads, fees and shipping.

What is the difference between margin and markup?

Margin is profit as a share of the selling price. Markup is profit as a share of your cost. A 50 percent markup equals a 33 percent margin.

Should I include shipping and fees?

Yes. Leaving them out makes your profit look bigger than it really is.
